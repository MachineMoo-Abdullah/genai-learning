# Meeting Summaries

## Prompt 1
```
Summarize this meeting for people who weren't there:

{{TRANSCRIPT_OR_NOTES}}

Output: a short summary, decisions made, action items as a table (owner/action/deadline — mark missing info explicitly), and open questions. Do not invent owners or deadlines.
```

## Prompt 2
```
Turn these raw meeting notes into a structured summary:

{{NOTES}}

Include: 2-3 sentence overview, bulleted decisions, action items with owners/deadlines, unresolved issues, and any notable disagreements. Keep it under one minute to read.
```
