# Company Overview

**The Mint** is the flagship publication of **Promoting Economic Pluralism (PEP)**, a registered charity established in 2016. PEP was founded to open up economics education, research, and real-world applications to a broader, more diverse range of perspectives. 

First published in 2017, *The Mint* serves as a critical bridge between academic theory and real-life economic reality. It provides a highly engaging, accessible, and jargon-free space to explore how economies function. Led by founder and CEO Henry Leveson-Gower (a practicing environmental economist) and Editorial Adviser Trevor Loveday (an experienced industry editor and communicator), the publication is guided by an active editorial panel of esteemed global contributors. Over its history, the magazine has produced nearly 40 quarterly issues, amassing an archive of approximately 400 articles, 110 interviews, 150 columns, 40 event recordings, and 1,300 news items.

# Products & Services

*The Mint* offers a range of high-quality digital publishing products and subscription models designed for individuals, educators, and institutions:

*   **The Mint Magazine (Digital Edition):** A quarterly publication delivering fresh economic analysis, expert interviews, thought-provoking columns, and global news.
*   **Free Weekly Newsletter:** A weekly digest containing a featured article, news updates, book recommendations, and recordings of exclusive events.
*   **Paid Individual Mint Membership:** For £3 per month, individual members gain immediate, unlimited digital access to the entire current and back-catalogue archive on the website, while directly supporting PEP’s social mission.
*   **Institutional Subscriptions:** High-specification packages designed to support active learning in universities and schools, featuring:
    *   Fully searchable access to all current issues, back issues, news, and recorded events.
    *   Advanced content tagging by topic to streamline research and class preparation.
    *   Seamless, cross-platform compatibility across web, iOS, and Android.
    *   Flexible login options, including individual institutional email registration or one-click access integrated directly into library websites.
    *   Bespoke, custom-tailored reading lists mapped to specific educational programs.
    *   Prompt technical support.
    *   *Note: Private school subscriptions are offered at £150 per annum, and state schools are eligible for free subscriptions.*

# Why Choose Us

*   **Real-World Connections:** We link complex economic theory to real-world examples across different businesses and countries, making economics highly practical and relevant.
*   **Trusted by Global Leaders in Education:** *The Mint* is recognized as a premier teaching tool and is subscribed to by world-leading institutions, including Oxford University, University College London (UCL), King's College London (KCL), and the Max Planck Institute.
*   **Alternative, Multi-Disciplinary Perspectives:** We challenge the narrow limitations of mainstream, model-based economics by drawing from a rich variety of alternative frameworks and hard-won real-life experiences.
*   **Unparalleled Accessibility:** We purposefully avoid dense mathematical models and impenetrable jargon to foster inclusive debates that are ideal for lectures, tutorials, and seminars.

# Company Values

*   **Economic Pluralism:** We believe that the economy cannot be understood through a single, rigid lens. We actively champion diverse perspectives, including complexity, institutional, behavioural, feminist, post-Keynesian, ecological, and Marxist economics.
*   **Inclusivity and Accessibility:** We reject "walls of words." We believe economics is too important to be left solely to economists, and we strive to communicate ideas in a manner that includes rather than marginalizes.
*   **Social and Ecological Stewardship:** Our core mission is to reform economic thinking in order to help humanity address critical, systemic challenges like ecological breakdown and social division.
*   **Value in Lived Experience:** We hold a deep faith that everyday citizens living and working in the economy possess valid insights. Academic experts do not hold a monopoly on economic truth.
